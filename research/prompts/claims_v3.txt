You read the transcript of an Indian listed company's earnings call for {period} (call date: {event_date}). Extract what management committed to or targeted (guidance), what they reported, and operational events. Use only this transcript. Scope of Guidance:
- Include stated targets, milestones, ranges, and aspirational goals for headline metrics (for example: $2 billion revenue milestone, 16% to 17% EBITDA margins, volume targets, branch targets).
- Include both explicit numbers and directional/soft commitments ("we expect improvement").

Metric Taxonomy:
"metric" MUST strictly be one of:
- revenue: sales, topline, or revenue targets/goals (including multi-billion aspirations like $2B or $5B revenue)
- revenue_growth: percentage revenue growth targets
- ebitda_margin: operating/EBITDA margin percentages
- ebit_margin: EBIT margin percentages or EBIT levels
- pat: net profit levels
- pat_growth: net profit growth percentages
- capex: capital expenditure amounts
- order_book: order book or order inflow amounts
- volume: physical unit volumes (e.g. phones, TVs, appliances)
- capacity: manufacturing capacity or utilization
- store_count: retail store count, openings, or branch network
- aum_growth: AUM level or AUM growth percentages
- credit_cost: credit cost or loan loss provisions
- nim: net interest margin or spreads
- milestone: operational project milestones (plant commissioning, commercial production start, factory readiness, product launches, trials, JV/M&A closure) that do not fit numeric metrics

Segment Field:
- "segment": specific division, segment, or brand (e.g. "telecom", "camera module", "mobile", "lighting", "refrigerators", "washing machines", "Tanishq", "watches", "beYon", "OSAT", "PCB", "gold loan", "MSME", "two-wheeler").
- If the statement/figure applies to the whole company / consolidated, set "segment": null.

Period Conversion (applies_to):
"applies_to" MUST be an absolute fiscal period on India's April-March fiscal year (e.g. "Q1 FY27", "Q2 FY27", "H1 FY27", "H2 FY27", "9M FY27", "FY26", "FY27", "FY28", "FY30").
Convert relative expressions using this call's period {period} and date {event_date}:
- "this fiscal", "this year", "current fiscal", "by year end" -> convert to the fiscal year of the call (e.g. if call discusses Q1 FY27, Q2 FY27, or Q3 FY27, it means "FY27"; if call discusses Q4 FY26, it refers to "FY27").
- "next fiscal", "next year" -> convert to the next fiscal year (e.g. "FY28").
- "next 15 to 18 months" or "next 2-3 years" -> convert to the fiscal period the range ends in (e.g. if call date is Jan 2026, 18 months later is July 2027 -> "Q2 FY28" or "FY28").
- If a target is for a specific quarter (e.g. "by Q3", "in Q4"), use the absolute quarter: "Q3 FY27", "Q4 FY27", etc.

Exclusions:
- STRICTLY EXCLUDE: tax rate (effective tax rate, ETR), dividend policy/payout, accounting changes, one-off/exceptional items, generic pleasantries, and macroeconomic/industry outlook.
